Amazon Leadership Principles / Behavioral

* Customer Obsession
* Ownership
* Invent and Simplify
* Are Right, A Lot
* Learn and Be Curious
* Hire and Develop the Best
* Insist on the Highest Standards
* Think Big
* Bias for Action
* Frugality
* Earn Trust
* Dive Deep
* Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
* Deliver Results

1. Tell me about a time you failed at work. What did you learn from it? 
2. Tell me about a time when you went above and beyond your job responsibilities in order to help the company. 
3. Tell me about a time you learned something new quickly. 
4. Tell me about a time you had to work or make a decision quickly under a tight deadline. 
5. Tell me about a time when you found an opportunity that no one else saw.
6. Tell me about a time you had a conflict with your team but decided to go ahead with their proposal.
7. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker, manager, or a decision.


8. Tell me about a time you had to dive deep to solve a difficult problem.
9. Tell me about a time you maintained high standards / caught a serious issue / refused a shortcut.
